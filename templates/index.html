Student Success Predictor

Estimates whether a student will drop out, graduate, or still be enrolled, from seven end of second semester signals. The form is pre-filled with a plausible student so you can predict straight away.

Trained on the UCI student dropout dataset. A statistical estimate over population averages, not advice about any individual student.
